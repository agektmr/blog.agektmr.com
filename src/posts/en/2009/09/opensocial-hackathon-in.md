I will be speaking at the [Aizu IT Summer Forum](http://www.ubic-u-aizu.pref.fukushima.jp/it-summer/2009overview), held at the University of Aizu on September 18th. In my session, I plan to cover a rather niche topic: "The Architecture of OpenSocial."

Also, taking advantage of the trip to Aizu, we will be hosting an OpenSocial Hackathon the following day on the 19th, which will also double as a caravan stop for [Mashup Awards5](http://mashupaward.jp/).

[Yoichiro Tanaka](http://www.eisbahn.jp/yoichiro/) from mixi, author of *Introduction to OpenSocial*, and [Yusuke Kawasaki](http://www.kawa.net/xp/index-j.html) from MTL will also be participating. This is a rare opportunity for locals, so please consider joining us!

Details and registration form: [OpenSocial Hackathon in Aizu](https://spreadsheets.google.com/a/agektmr.com/viewform?formkey=dGtZbVFaSzNhbTdZb1RPd3h3azhHSmc6MA..)
