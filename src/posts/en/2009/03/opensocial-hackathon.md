Timed nicely with the launch of the <a target="_blank" href="http://developer.home.goo.ne.jp/">goo Home Sandbox</a>, goo, the <a target="_blank" href="http://groups.google.com/group/opensocial-japan">OpenSocial Japan Community</a>, and <a target="_blank" href="http://mtl.recruit.co.jp/">Recruit Media Technology Lab</a> are co-hosting an <a target="_blank" href="http://groups.google.com/group/opensocial-hackathon-0424">OpenSocial Hackathon</a>.

A hackathon is a portmanteau of "hack" and "marathon," where participants are divided into groups and build software by coding right on the spot.

A few hackathons have been organized in the past, primarily by Google, but this time around we'll be building on top of the <a target="_blank" href="http://sandbox.home.goo.ne.jp/">goo Home Sandbox</a> as our container.

* * *

**Organizers:** goo Home, Recruit Media Technology Lab, OpenSocial-Japan Community  
**Date & Time:** Friday, April 24, 2009, 10:00–18:00 (Doors open at 09:30)  
**Location:** Recruit Media Technology Lab  
**Capacity:** 35 attendees  
**Requirements:**

*   Interested in OpenSocial
*   Experience programming in JavaScript
*   Experience developing OpenSocial gadgets  
    (Going through the tutorial below will help you get up to speed smoothly)  
    <http://code.google.com/intl/ja/apis/opensocial/articles/tutorial/tutorial-0.8.html>
*   Able to bring your own laptop

*Note: Participants are also asked to attend a preliminary meeting on April 14.*  
**Date & Time:** Tuesday, April 14, 2009, 18:30–20:30 (Doors open at 18:00)  
**Location:** Recruit Media Technology Lab  
**Agenda:** Team formation and logistics for the Hackathon, brainstorming ideas

* * *
