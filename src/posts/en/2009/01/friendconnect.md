I developed [FriendIntroducer][1] as an experiment to understand how FriendConnect differs from ordinary OpenSocial implementations. This time, I tried developing a gadget that showcases what makes FriendConnect interesting: Footprints. If you've used MyBlogLog before, you'll already be familiar with the concept.

## What is Footprints?

Footprints is a gadget that tracks visitors to your blog. Take a look at the gadget at the bottom left. If you haven't joined or signed in yet, try doing so to see how it works.

<img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-326" title="Footprints1" src="/images/2009/01/e38394e382afe38381e383a3-5.png" alt="Footprints1" width="223" height="211" />

As you might imagine, this gadget records visitors and their visit times. When viewed by others, it displays a timestamp showing how long ago you visited. You can also remove your own footprint if you wish. The XML is located at:

<span style="text-decoration: line-through;">http://devlab.agektmr.com/OpenSocial/FriendConnect/Footprints.xml</span>

<a href="http://gadgets.agektmr.com/Footprints/friendconnect.xml" target="_blank">http://gadgets.agektmr.com/Footprints/friendconnect.xml</a>

Feel free to grab it and use it on your blog.
