In addition to [Orkut](http://www.orkut.com/Home.aspx), [hi5](http://hi5.com/), and [MySpace](http://www.myspace.com/), new OpenSocial containers like [iGoogle](http://www.google.com/ig?hl=en), [hyves](http://www.hyves.nl/), and [Netlog](http://en.netlog.com/) have started to appear.

While each social network has its own unique characteristics, looking up specifications one by one and verifying behavior during development can be a hassle. Since OpenSocial v0.8 has only just been released, most containers currently support v0.7, but there is a gadget available that lets you check their compliance with v0.7.

http://opensocial-resources.googlecode.com/svn/tests/trunk/compliancetests.xml


![compliancetest](/images/2008/06/compliancetest-257x299.jpg)

This should make development at least a little bit easier.
