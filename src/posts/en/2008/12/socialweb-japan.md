Realizing that there wasn't really a community hosting study sessions around the social web, I went ahead and created <a href="http://groups.google.com/group/socialweb-japan" target="_blank">SocialWeb Japan</a> on Google Groups.

> SocialWeb Japan is a community aimed at exchanging information and organizing study sessions around social web technologies, focusing on areas like OpenStack and Facebook.
> 
> If any of these keywords resonate with you, please feel free to join.
> 
> *   OpenID
> *   OAuth
> *   OpenSocial
> *   PortableContacts
> *   XRDS-Simple
> *   Google FriendConnect
> *   Facebook Connect
> *   MySpaceID
> *   Yahoo! Open Strategy
> *   etc&#8230;
